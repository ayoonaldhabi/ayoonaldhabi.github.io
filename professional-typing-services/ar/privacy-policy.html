An overview of the information we may collect, why we use it, and the measures we take to protect it.

Depending on the service, we may receive information such as your name, phone number, email address, service request, messages and documents or details you choose to provide. Certain government or business services may require additional personal or document information.

2. How We Use Information

Where applicable, you may contact us to request correction or clarification of information you have provided. Certain records may need to be retained to meet legal, regulatory, contractual or operational requirements.
